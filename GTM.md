# Dura Mater GTM

## Core message

**Let agents do the disposable work. Keep the judgment that makes you good.**

Dura Mater is Garmin for cognitive fitness. It shows programmers how they use coding agents, which skills they still exercise, and where they may be giving away too much judgment.

## Initial user

Vibe coders who use Codex or Claude Code every day and care about becoming more capable, not merely shipping more code.

## Activation

One command installs local Codex and Claude hooks. Dura Mater begins observing sessions automatically.

The first value arrives after one real task: a short recap of what the agent did, what the user changed, and which decisions the user kept.

## Viral artifact

A weekly card with facts that can be verified from session history:

> 11 important decisions
>
> You reviewed 8 and changed 3
>
> 3 were accepted without review
>
> Judgment streak: 6 days

No invented intelligence score. The facts should be interesting enough to share.

Sharing exposes the product; peers install it to see their own pattern. Better history produces better recaps, which gives users a reason to keep it running.

## Launch

Recruit 20 respected terminal-agent users. Give them a private weekly recap, improve it from their reactions, then let them publish their cards on X, Hacker News, and developer communities.

Lead with their real findings, not the product philosophy.

## First experiment

Install Dura Mater for 20 users for two weeks.

Success: at least 8 check their recap in week two without a personal reminder, and at least 4 share a fact or invite another user.
